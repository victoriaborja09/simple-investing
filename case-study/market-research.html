01 / PARTICIPATION

A real market gap, not linear growth.

Investing surged among younger adults through 2021, then lost ground by 2024. The latest participation data challenge the assumption that access alone steadily increases adoption.

05 / IMPLICATIONS FOR SIMPLE INVESTING

The opportunity is fewer decisions, not another brokerage.

External research supports studying how interested but hesitant younger adults begin investing. It does not establish demand for any specific three-ETF preset portfolio; that needs direct user testing.

01

Reduce the first investment decision

Test whether one clearly explained ETF portfolio improves time-to-understanding and onboarding completion versus a guided alternative.

02

Build trust through visibility

Explain what's inside the portfolio, fees, volatility and downside risk before a user chooses a recurring contribution.

03

Validate with actual customers

Compare preference, comprehension, trust and willingness to start among 18–24-year-olds, particularly interested non-investors.
